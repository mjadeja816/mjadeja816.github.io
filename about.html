I am Dr. Mahipal Jadeja, an educator and researcher at MNIT Jaipur. My work connects rigorous computational ideas with practical, accessible learning.

The connecting thread

Understanding first.
Possibilities follow.

My research foundations lie in theoretical computer science, graph theory, and graph neural networks. Working with networks means asking how individual pieces connect—and what those connections help us understand.

That perspective now also shapes my contributions to Generative AI education. I build learning tools, share open resources, and work with educators and professionals to turn new capabilities into thoughtful practice.

I serve as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Malaviya National Institute of Technology Jaipur. Alongside research and supervision, I am interested in how multilingual explanations, useful examples, and interactive experiences can make learning more accessible.
