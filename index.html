About my journey

From the theory of graphs to the practice of learning.

My foundations are in theoretical computer science, graph theory, and graph neural networks. Today, that same curiosity shapes how I think about Generative AI: not just what it can produce, but how it can help people understand.

At MNIT Jaipur and through open educational resources, I connect research, classroom practice, and accessible technology.
