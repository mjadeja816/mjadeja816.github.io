The checkbook problem, step by step

The solution first removes the office possibility, then uses the remaining breakfast possibility to locate the checkbook.

Original Statements

  1. 1
    I was looking at the phone bill for payment at breakfast or in my office.
  2. 2
    If I was looking at the phone bill at breakfast, then the checkbook is on the breakfast table.
  3. 3
    If I was looking at the phone bill in my office, then the checkbook is on my office table.
  4. 4
    I did not pay my phone bill.
  5. 5
    If my checkbook is on my office table, then I paid my phone bill.
