# The AI Family Mini Lab

A two-window web mini app for Module 1.

## Windows

- Explore AI: distinguishes Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, and Large Language Models through one smart campus scenario.
- AI Through Time: uses an interactive timeline to connect major AI milestones with the same conceptual hierarchy.
